Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

CAL is designed as a client-side calculator. This policy describes what the current GitHub Pages version does with information you provide while using the site.

1. Information we collect

CAL does not require an account, name, email address, or other personal information to use the calculator. Calculator inputs and results are processed in your browser.

2. Local preferences

CAL may store settings such as sound, vibration, animation, and compact-mode preferences in your browser's local storage. These preferences remain on your device unless you clear the site's stored data.

3. Third-party services

The calculator may load fonts from Google Fonts and the Anime.js library from cdnjs. Those services may receive standard technical information associated with a web request. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to those requests.

4. GitHub Pages

CAL is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request data as part of providing the hosting service. See GitHub's applicable privacy documentation for details.

5. Cookies

CAL does not intentionally use advertising or tracking cookies. Browser storage may be used for local calculator preferences as described above.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CAL's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

7. Contact

If you have a privacy question about CAL, use the contact method provided by the repository owner.
